Education Support

Helping disadvantaged children access and stay in school — with scholarships, materials, tuition help, literacy and computer education.

Education program

Keeping children learning

Children Intergrated Missions supports disadvantaged children through scholarships, school supplies, tuition support, homework assistance, literacy programmes and computer education. We also create academic forums where school-going children can interact, learn and participate in competitive debate.

Our goal is simple: remove the material and financial barriers that push vulnerable children out of school, and give them the reading, writing and digital foundations they need to compete and collaborate academically.

Objectives

01

Stay enrolled

Keep vulnerable children enrolled and learning in school.

02

Close gaps

Close material and tuition gaps that block access to education.

03

Literacy & digital

Build literacy and digital foundations for future opportunity.

What we do

1
Scholarships and tuition support
School fees and tuition help so vulnerable children can enrol and remain in school.
2
School supplies and learning materials
School supplies, uniforms, educational materials and everyday learning needs.
3
Homework assistance and tutoring
Homework help, tutoring, mentorship and encouragement to keep learning on track.
4
Literacy, computer education and academic forums
Literacy programmes, computer education, and forums where children interact, learn and debate.

Expected impact

  • Better school attendance and retention
  • Stronger reading, writing and digital skills
  • Children able to compete and collaborate academically
"Education opens the door — our role is to make sure vulnerable children can walk through it and stay in the room."
— CIMSL education approach
