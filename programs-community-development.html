Community Development

Working with families, schools, churches, government institutions and local communities to improve child welfare and strengthen protection systems.

Community

Strong communities protect children

Children thrive when the people and institutions around them work together. Children Integrated Missions partners with families, schools, churches, government institutions and local communities to improve child welfare and strengthen child protection systems.

Our approach is community-rooted and practical: family engagement, school and church partnerships, collaboration with councils, ministries and local authorities, and awareness of child rights and referral pathways.

  • Families supported and engaged in child welfare
  • Schools and churches as active protection partners
  • Government institutions and local authorities engaged
  • Child rights awareness and referral pathways
  • Stronger community-based protection systems
Objectives

What this program aims to achieve

01

Stronger family & community care

Support families and local communities to protect and nurture vulnerable children.

02

Protection systems that work

Strengthen child welfare and protection systems with schools, churches and institutions.

03

Rights, laws & accountability

Promote child rights awareness and engagement with decision makers and authorities.

Activities

How we work

1
Family engagement
Work with families to improve care, wellbeing and opportunities for vulnerable children.
2
School & church partnerships
Partner with schools and churches on welfare, mentorship, learning and protection.
3
Councils, ministries & local authorities
Engage district councils, line ministries and local authorities so children receive the benefits and protections intended for them.
4
Child-rights & referral awareness
Raise awareness among children of their rights, responsibilities and referral pathways when rights are violated.
Expected impact

Communities that sustain change

When families are engaged, schools and churches are involved, and institutions play their part, protection becomes part of everyday community life — not a one-time intervention. This work sustains our care, education and health programs over time.
