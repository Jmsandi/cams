Who we are

A mission built around vulnerable children

Children Integrated Missions (CIM) is committed to restoring hope and creating opportunities for orphaned, abandoned, vulnerable and disadvantaged children.

Our work goes beyond meeting immediate needs. We support children's holistic development through education, healthcare, protection, mentorship, practical life skills and community-based interventions.

  • Quality care and emotional support
  • Educational support and mentorship
  • Healthcare, nutrition and hygiene

Why Support CIMSL?

02

Our impact framework

Building a Better Tomorrow

Six objectives guide everything we do — from hygiene supplies to advocacy with decision makers.

01

Child welfare & hygiene

Seek the welfare of children through provision of hygienic supplies.

02

Child protection advocacy

Engage decision makers to formulate and enforce laws protecting children.

03

Academic development

Academic forums where children interact, learn and join competitive debate.

04

Child rights awareness

Help children know their rights, responsibilities and referral pathways.

05

Institutional collaboration

Work with ministries, councils and local authorities so children receive what is intended for them.

06

Support for marginalised children

Identify vulnerable children and open opportunities that improve their lives.
