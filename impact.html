Our Impact

An honest framework for change across child protection, education, healthcare and community development — built on CIMSL's six real objectives.

Impact statistics coming soon. We do not publish unverified numbers — no invented children-served counts, donation totals, or success percentages. The qualitative impact below reflects our real areas of work.
Impact framework

Our six objectives

01

Child welfare & hygiene

Seek the welfare of children through provision of hygienic supplies.

02

Child protection advocacy

Engage decision makers to formulate and enforce laws protecting children.

03

Academic development

Academic forums where school-going children interact, learn and join competitive debate.

04

Child rights awareness

Raise awareness of rights, responsibilities and referral pathways when rights are violated.

05

Institutional collaboration

Engage ministries, district councils and local authorities so children receive intended benefits and protections.

06

Support for marginalised children

Identify marginalised and vulnerable children and provide life-improving opportunities.

Safeguarding

Protection is the foundation

Every activity — from tutoring to health outreach — is shaped by safeguarding: children's safety, dignity and privacy come first. We never publish identifying or sensitive information about children without proper authorisation, and we anonymise where appropriate.

"A child who feels safe can learn, heal and dream. Protection is not one program among many — it underlies everything we do."
— CIMSL approach

Education & healthcare

  • Better school attendance and retention
  • Stronger reading, writing and digital skills
  • Healthier children with fewer preventable illnesses
  • Better nutrition and hygiene habits
  • Earlier identification of health and learning needs

Community & future priorities

Stronger family and community ownership of child welfare, better coordination around vulnerable children, and deeper partnership with schools, faith groups and local authorities.

  • Expand literacy and academic forums
  • Deepen health, nutrition and hygiene outreach
  • Strengthen safeguarding systems with communities
  • Grow volunteer and partnership networks
