## 1. PROJECT OBJECTIVE

Completely redesign and modernize the existing CIMSL website:

**Current website:** https://cimsl.org/

**Target theme:** https://themewagon.github.io/fundraiser/

Instead:

> **Preserve and professionally restructure the real CIMSL content, mission, programs, objectives, donation information, contact information and organizational identity inside the modern Fundraiser theme architecture.**

The final website should look like a professionally designed international NGO/non-profit organization website rather than a basic WordPress/template website.

The website should communicate:

* Trust
* Professionalism
* Transparency
* Child protection
* Community impact
* Hope
* Humanitarian work
* Accountability
* Partnership
* Opportunities for donors and volunteers

The final result should be visually comparable to a modern international NGO website while remaining authentic to CIMSL and Sierra Leone.

# 2. IMPORTANT IMPLEMENTATION RULE

Do NOT simply replace the Fundraiser theme's Lorem Ipsum with CIMSL content.

Instead:

1. Study the current CIMSL website.
